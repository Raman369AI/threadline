# Code of conduct

We want participation in Threadline to be respectful, constructive, and safe.

Contributors should communicate clearly, critique ideas and code rather than people, respect differing experience levels, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or disclosure of another person's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior disrupts the project. Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer. Reports will be handled with discretion and reviewed as promptly as practical.
